# Juice

Added a couple of quicky impact sounds.

Tried a [camerashake script](https://gist.github.com/ftvs/5822103) but it didn't add anything good, felt less funny (is it still funny?)

Considered adding a possibility of shattering the model with a plugin, but the one I found for URP was like $50 and does the idea that this is a brittle model sync with the rubbery dents being created right now? Maybe if some dust or shards came off in the initial dents but that seems like a lot of polish.

Do I even like this? I like that I was able to get deformation happening and I enjoyed reading the research material and hooking things up.

I wonder, the first prototype ([Chiromancer](https://github.com/wysiwyggins/Chiromancer)) Was sort of direct manipulation of character with a lot of control and granularity in the features being changed but then there's a sort of doomed faith leap off of a cliff when you realize that you don't know what any of the features do, and then eventually, it doesn't matter what they do, because fate is inescapable.

In this prototype, you can only subtract from character. The only agency you have over it is to ruin it. You never get to see what these numbers are good for, other than maybe having pre-knowledge that they are common role-playing game character stats.

Is there something more here or should I move on? Maybe some timing tweaks? Maybe the stats don't instantly appear but have some game show delayed reveal? maybe even like some little pachinko noises as they get set?
