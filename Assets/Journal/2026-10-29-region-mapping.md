Ok let's see...

it seems like these numbers on the [phrenology chart](2026-10-28-research.md) are bilaterally symmetrical

'Wit' corresponds to upper right and upper left, so I could have either of those affect intelligence.

I was really hoping there would be a region that was like 'kinesthetic sense' or something for things like strength and dexterity. Wisdom, charaisma, I don't know. I may just have to pick arbitrary regions and then lean on the german text on the head to make it a little more ambiguous to non German readers. Otherwise the whole thing is, "haha, they got the wrong locations but summarizing a person in five numbers between 0 and 20 is totally normal."

I don't think I can do much of a mapping that makes sense because there aren't many strong correspondances and also quite a few of the regions are on other parts of the head and I don't want to have to allow rotating around the head to take a punch. I'd have to extend the curtain all the way around which I guess is fine but I like having a fixed view and a limited number of spots to hit the head.

These kinds of prototypes seem like good ideas when you first have them and then as you start to make them they don't always seem like such good ideas any more, because I can't help but think "what am I saying/doing here" as I am forced to pick out how it works mechanically, each design decision feels like a statement rather than just bubblegum and tape holding the set decorations together— this is because I'm granting transparency into the process and now the process is part of the piece.

I think like the palm reading there is going to be a relationship between the head and the output but it won't be predictable, and if players react the same way to the same sort of setup, there will be the dissapointment of trying to figure out a relationship and having no moment of figuring it out.

Maybe I will try to get an early playtest and move on to another prototype, these two are very similar because I had the ideas at the same time, it's enough to have tried them both.

I don't see a clear and useable mapping yet and translating individual labels on the model isn't giving great results so far.

These incredibly complex systems seem like they grow out of the fear of, or maybe inability to guess the intentions of a stranger. It's like a way to deal with the urban shock of being in close proximity to too many unknown people, or having business dealings with strangers that are inherently adversarial in a hidden way rather than social/familial dealings. There's a detective aspect— the face and hands as identifiers, leaving traces, the criminology connection to physiognomy was strong.

Ok, I found translated versions of what look to be the Carl Huter physiognomic maps. I won't add them here because there are still people practicing this stuff and selling materials, (theres an iphone app!)

It looks like they have both a mapping from parts of the face to the organs in some kind of reflexology/accupuncture-style thing and another map to character traits or faculties, and of course it seems arbitrary compared to other systems.

I'm looking at my model and seeing if there are correspondances, and I found a wonderful thing. Directly on the tip of the nose of my model, where on my translated map I see "mind," the model has "erlebnis," which is a work I know from Benjamin. Erlebnis isn't mind, it's experience, and it's the kind of experience that not useful to the experiencer,

from my notes
> *Erlebnis* is fundamentally individual, *Erfahrung* (traditionally a learned experience) is used by Benjamin as a collective experience. *Erfahrung* corresponds to active production, while *Erlebnis* mirrors passive consumption.

It also brings to mind the "Shock experience" *chockerlebnis* which Benjamin talks about a lot when writing about Baudelaire, in this case it could be the shock experience of being punched in the nose.

Some possible D&D stat correspondances on these maps- Memory is shown as upper middle forehead. "HEART AND BODY STRENGTH" are below the nose and above the mouth to the sides where the smile lines are. Any of thee could be wisdom, but the side temples seem to have things like judgement which could be a good connection. Strength is listed on the chin. Vitality on the side of the cheek.
