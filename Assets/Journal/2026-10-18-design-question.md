# Design Question

![An 'honest' face](../Textures/honest.jpg)

This is project start for "phrenologist": another small prototype about character creation. This follows "chiromancer."

This is my first time back in Unity in maybe two years? Once again I am starting with an idea that is a sort of comedy beat that I can imagine. I'm wanting to create another association like I did in chiromancer. This one won't be directly connected to ideas of fate, however, but more closely working with ideas of quantifying people and all that goes with that, and the reliance on these sort of quantitative attributes for computability in games. So once again I'm not sure that I am starting with a doubt or a design question I can put into words other than "how do these similar things relate? One of which I value pretty negatively and one that just seems inherent to certain types of games."

My biggest unknown on this project is technical. I am going to try to simulate punching and deforming a model mesh, which is not something I have ever done in unity. I found a catlike coding page with some information about how to do it: <https://catlikecoding.com/unity/tutorials/mesh-deformation/>

![German phrenology head](../Textures/head.png)

I've already found a really beautiful and texture detailed phrenology bust on Turbosquid, and made a dramatic theater curtain in blender as a backdrop (I haven't done any 3D modeling since I was in school at UCLA and most of the tools I used to use I have been without access to, most noticably Substance Painter, which I used to love using but was bought by Adobe and assigned to a high priced creative cloud tier.)

For anyone unfamiliar, phrenology is a soundly disproven 19th century pseudoscience which posited that skull measurements could be mapped to character traits, closely associated with physiognomy, which related more specifically to face types and character. The [wikipedia page on phrenology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phrenology) does a good job of outlining its history.

My idea is another character creation screen for a nonexistent videogame. Whacking and deforming a phrenology bust rolls classic dungeons and dragons style character stats with each hit. It might be nice to even have the bust teeter a bit at times. I'm unsure what the interaction will feel like. Does kicking feel like a hit? maybe another object could be swung into the head, like a bat, changing the interaction from a click to a click and drag. I suppose it could also be VR, which would make the interaction an actual punch, but I don't particularly feel like engaging with VR at this point in this prototype. There could also be a generate character button that simply plays one of a few different animations of the bust being hit, either by a visible or invisible thing.
