# The Shock Experience

A traumatic first encounter with a mirror,
a failed christening,
a violent, strangled laugh.
A disassembling of moments like a string of beads popped loose to flee from one another
(It all amounts to nothing.)

Dungeons and Dragons style stats are unusual. They're supposed to be traits, something like what the palm-reader describes, but wisdom isn't a trait, it's something accumulated, isn't it?

The shock experience is the experience that can't be assimilated, it must be protected against, it causes a sort of atrophy of experience. It's sitting on the bus looking at your shoes thinking about a grocery list while a person at the back of the bus howls and screams at an imaginary person, tearing at their clothes and hitting themselves in the face.

It's waking up and planning to take a flight back home for your birthday and reading disconnected rumours on social media that the US president is planning on launching a nuclear attack against another country, and then walking into the bathroom to brush your teeth as a large centipede mills around in the bathtub looking for a way out.

Maybe I will start wisdom at 0.
