# Chiromancer

![Create Your Character](/Assets/Textures/3F4B5CD1-A3CB-49DC-8EB1-7421CB5A371A-734-00017BA46F97FBAD.png)

A (subtractive) character creator built in Unity.

## Why

This is (possibly the last) in a few experiments related to videogame character creators and associated ideas outside of games. The previous experiment was related to the association between the ideas of character and fate. This experiment is related to ideas of character and the body, as in the pseudoscience of phrenology/physiognomy. This association seems to come later than the ideas of character and fate used by the mantic reader or fortune teller, but both make similar claims of inherited authority for their claims that seem to flail from one source to another, struggling to adapt to changing conceptions of self, mind and history.

Also it's just kind of funny to punch a poor, defenseless bust.

## Conclusion

As with [Chiromancer](https://github.com/wysiwyggins/Chiromancer), figuring out *how* to do a thing was also discovering what it means. I hadn't preconcieved of how whacking a phrenology head would produce rpg/videogame character stats, just that the action of striking or deforming the head would produce them in a simple, funny beat. Figuring out the regions of the head and how physiognomy might map to rpg stats took some consideration and led to another fun research rabbit hole. The idea that the character might start in a close to ideal state and then be whittled down to a set of diminished numbers was arrived at, but after being implemented seemed meaningful. The production of character here is violent, and nothing is gained from it.

Reflective journaling again gave me a place to talk about the reading that fueled the project, and gave me a hole to shout down into when I wasn't sure what I was doing or how to do it.
